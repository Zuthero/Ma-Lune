# Safety design / Sécurité

## How Ma Lune handles danger
1. In every place where a girl can type freely, Ma Lune checks her message for danger words.
2. If one is found, Ma Lune sends a safety message (go to the nearest health centre or a trusted adult if in danger now) and opens a private **Urgence** ticket.
3. A trained team member replies in the chat. Target: same day, at most 24 hours.
4. The AI assistant never answers questions about violence, abuse, pregnancy, self-harm, heavy bleeding or any danger. It replies `HUMAIN`, and the flow hands the conversation to a person.

## Privacy
- Consent is asked before anything else.
- Only a nickname, age group, language, period dates and answers are stored.
- Users can ask for deletion with the word SUPPRIMER or by email.
- Under-13s use Ma Lune with a parent, on the parent's phone.
- Data is deleted 12 months after the last message, or within 30 days after SUPPRIMER.
